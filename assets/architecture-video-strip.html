Listen to the episode gemini-3.7-flash
The model listens to the actual audio and writes one plain sentence per window: what the hosts are talking about during those 16 seconds.
Design one scene per story, then an action per window gemma-4-31b-it
Gemma invents a single scene for each story - the place, the mannequin figure, the props, one accent color. Then, for every window inside that story, it writes what the mannequin is doing at that moment, based on what the hosts are saying right then. Same scene throughout a story; the action follows the conversation.
Render one clip per window veo-3.1-fast
Each window's description becomes its own unique 8-second video clip. All clips share one look: smooth, faceless mannequin figures (no eyes, no mouth, no hair) in photorealistic scenes.
Assemble the video code · ffmpeg
Each 8-second clip is slowed down to cover its full 16-second window. Clips fade into one another, the scene changes exactly when the hosts move to a new story, and the episode audio is laid on top.
