Listen to the episode gemini-3.7-flash
The model listens to the actual audio and writes one plain sentence per window: what the hosts are talking about during those ten or so seconds.
One scene per story, one action per window gemma-4-31b-it
Gemma invents a single scene for each story - the place, the mannequin figure, the props, one accent color. Then, for every window inside that story, it writes what the mannequin is doing at that moment, based on what the hosts are saying right then.
Render one clip per window veo-3.1-fast
Each window's description becomes its own unique 8-second clip. All clips share one look: smooth, faceless mannequin figures - no eyes, no mouth, no hair.
Assemble the video code · ffmpeg
Each clip is slowed down to cover its full window and fades in and out, the scene changes exactly when the hosts move to a new story, and the episode audio is laid on top.
